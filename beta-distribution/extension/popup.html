How does VaultBubble work?

VaultBubble helps you save webpages, text, and notes for your own reference.

To help organize and structure saved content, VaultBubble may send selected text and page URLs to off-site services that perform automated analysis. These services are asked not to use your data for training or other secondary purposes.

VaultBubble does not sell or share your data for economic benefit. Saved content is stored locally on your device unless you choose to connect a VaultBubble account, in which case it is stored under your account.

Any metrics or estimates produced are heuristic and informational only. They do not measure accuracy, truthfulness, credibility, or trustworthiness.
