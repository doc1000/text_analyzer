Phase 1 of the Vault Bubbles Data Orchard consumer code lives in `app/dataorchard/` (ids, capture with CaptureWriter, copy loaders plus synthetic scale, packed_tree adapter, lazy FastAPI router that is not mounted, and a NotImplementedError store seam), with tests in `tests/dataorchard/` (10 passing, no fastapi/DB needed). The real extension sends `{url,title,text,mode,tags,captured_at,pdf_to_parse,linked_pdf_urls}`, not `full_text/highlight/notes`; `normalize_payload` maps `text` to `full_text` for page mode, to `highlight` for selection mode (text after `[captured]`), and to `notes` for note mode. Nothing in app/main.py, models, db, migrations or extension was touched, and no database was contacted.
