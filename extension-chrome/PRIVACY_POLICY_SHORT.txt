Privacy Policy for VaultBubble

VaultBubble collects website content (text, URLs, page titles) and user-entered data (notes, tags) when you explicitly capture content. This data is sent only to your local vault server (localhost:8000) and stored on your computer by default. Data is used to organize content into topics and enable search functionality.

VaultBubble does not sell, share, or transmit your data to external servers unless you configure optional cloud storage. You control what you capture and can delete content at any time. All data remains private and local unless you choose otherwise.
